# What this demo proves

Jev receives the full present-tense token screen: contract controls, taxes, liquidity and removal history, creator and verified-vesting holdings, trading flow, and failed sells. The generator keeps the future outcome outside the state.

Honeypot recall is its own headline measure. Failed sell attempts only become reliable when buys work and almost no sell succeeds; one failure among hundreds of successful sells remains ordinary network noise.

The 18 decoys test causal reading. Concentration in a verified vesting contract is not creator control, and an old unlocked pool that has never moved is not the same as liquidity being removed.

Every token, ticker, address, pool and trade history is invented. This is a screening exercise, not investment advice.
